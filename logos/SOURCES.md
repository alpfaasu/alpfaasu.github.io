# Where each logo came from

Logos are trademarks of their owners. They appear on this site only to say
that a chapter member worked at that firm (referential use), never to imply
sponsorship. "Public domain" on Wikimedia Commons means the artwork is below
the threshold of originality, not that the trademark is free.

Collected 2026-10-06. Firms already here before that date (Deloitte, EY, PwC,
KPMG, Schwab, Amex, Intel, Honeywell, Freeport, Republic Services) came from
Wikimedia Commons and Simple Icons, see CLAUDE.md.

## Sizing, 2026-10-06

Every logo is cropped tight to its artwork (PNG margins trimmed; padded SVG
viewBoxes tightened on Honeywell Aerospace, MUFG, Intel, RDF) and every page
draws logos with object-fit: contain inside a fixed box, so a logo fills the
box and sits centred whatever its shape. If a new logo looks small, check its
file for empty margin before touching the CSS.
