A logo wall tells you a firm hired somebody once. It does not tell you who, or how, or whether they would take your call. Pick a firm and you get the people who have worked there, what they did, where they went next, and a way to ask them for twenty minutes without having to work out how to introduce yourself.

If you have never done this

A coffee chat is not a favour you are begging for.

  1. This is the chapter's own listThese are people who came through ALPFA at ASU and whose details the chapter keeps so members can reach them. The awkward part, working out whether it is allowed, is already done.
  2. Ask for twenty minutesNot an hour, not a referral, not a job. Twenty minutes about how they got there. The button writes the message for you.
  3. Come with a questionSomething you could not have answered by reading their LinkedIn. That is the whole difference between a good chat and a wasted one.
