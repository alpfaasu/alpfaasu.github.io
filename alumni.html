A logo wall tells you a firm hired somebody once. It does not tell you who, or how, or whether they would take your call. These are the people, what they did here, what they do now, and a way to ask them for twenty minutes without having to work out how to introduce yourself.

If you have never done this

A coffee chat is not a favour you are begging for.

  1. They said yes alreadyNobody is listed here who has not agreed to be asked. The awkward part, working out whether it is allowed, is already done.
  2. Ask for twenty minutesNot an hour, not a referral, not a job. Twenty minutes about how they got there. The button writes the message for you.
  3. Come with one real questionSomething you could not have answered by reading their LinkedIn. That is the whole difference between a good chat and a wasted one.
